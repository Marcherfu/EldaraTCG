## Lore

In the land of Eldara, a place where magical forces are in perfect balance and harmony, something happened. A disaster. A cataclysmic event known as... the Shattering.
Nobody knows the nature of the Shattering, but everyone knows how it affected the landscape : everybody got separated and started to try to survive without their old allies, whatever the cost.

The righteous and strong Angels of the Luminae Order started to hunt everybody who would do something considered as a criminal offense by their own laws.

The Drakarian Clans and their absolute leader started to throw flames at each other, trying to become the new great Unifier of the Clans by pure Strength.

The Abyssal Court, lead by their Queen, started going even deeper into the seas, working mostly on their paperwork in order to make excavation teams to search and find powerful artifacts buried by the Shattering.

The Verdant Coalition started to threathen everyone who would try to enter their Forest. Putting natural boundaries around it and trying to grow a peaceful place in it, only for them.

And lastly, those who were the most affected by the Shattering. Those who lost everything : the Shattered Remnants. We don't know much about them, but they certainly are peoples who have gone mad and on a dark path. Some peoples think that a Necromancer among them might be behind the Shattering. But whatever peoples says, only one thing is certain : they are crazy and would use even the darkest magics to get to their goal.

And you in all of that ? Well, you're a Contractor. You're doing allegiance contracts with those factions, trying to work things out and make the whole world a better place like before the Shattering.

Who you'll stand for is a decision only you can take. Good luck Contractor !


## Lore concept

The lore of the game is planned to evolve accross the expansions, with right now Core Set 1 being the very start of the story. We are currently some time after the Shattering, when things have settled on a daily war between the 5 Factions. What would happen during the expansion could very much shape the story going forward.
